🐶 Origin Story

Born in the neon-lit forests of the digital realm, Squicky was no ordinary squirrel. While others hoarded acorns, Squicky collected something far more valuable: knowledge of privacy, performance, and open-source tools. After witnessing countless users lose their data to greedy trackers, Squicky embarked on a quest to build a sanctuary of free, private web tools that anyone can use without fear.
