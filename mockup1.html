Meet Squicky

The story behind the squirrel and the mission that drives us.

A Squirrel with a Mission

Squicky started as a simple idea: what if web tools could be powerful AND private? Named after the clever squirrel who collects and protects its treasures, Squicky.in is a growing collection of browser-based tools that keep your data exactly where it belongs - on your device.

Every tool in the Squicky ecosystem processes files locally in your browser. No server uploads, no data collection, no accounts required. Just open, use, and close. Your files stay yours.

From our universal media player that handles 14+ formats to our comprehensive PDF toolkit with 9 specialized tools, we are building the privacy-first web toolkit you deserve.
