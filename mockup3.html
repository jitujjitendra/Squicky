The Squicky Story

Squicky started as a simple idea: what if web tools actually respected the people using them? No tracking scripts, no server uploads, no dark patterns. Just tools that work.

Every Squicky tool processes data entirely in your browser. Your files, your media, your documents - they stay on your device. We never see them, never store them, never sell them.

We are building a suite of tools and games that prove you do not have to sacrifice privacy for quality. The web platform is incredibly powerful, and we are harnessing it to create experiences that are both delightful and respectful.
